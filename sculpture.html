SCULPTURE

Explore five remarkable sculptures and discover how artists use form, texture, space, and balance to express ideas.

THE COLLECTION

Six Sculptures

Each artwork offers a different way of understanding three-dimensional art.

01 / 06

The Thinker

Auguste Rodin

1904 version • Bronze

The Thinker shows a seated man deep in thought. It symbolizes intelligence, reflection, and the power of the human mind.

02 / 06

David

Michelangelo

1501–1504 • Marble

David is a famous Renaissance sculpture that depicts the biblical hero David. It represents courage, strength, and human beauty.

03 / 06

Venus De Milo

Unknown ancient Greek sculptor

150–125 BCE • Marble

Venus de Milo is an ancient Greek sculpture believed to represent Aphrodite, the goddess of love and beauty. It is famous for its graceful pose and missing arms.

04 / 06

Victoire de Samothrace

Unknown ancient Greek sculptor.

Approximately 190 BCE • Marble

This sculpture depicts Nike, the Greek goddess of victory, with wings spread as if landing on a ship. It represents triumph, movement, and power.

05 / 06

Rodin Le Baiser

Auguste Rodin

1882 • Marble

The Kiss portrays two lovers in a close embrace. It expresses love, passion, and human connection through smooth marble carving.

06 / 06

The Little Mermaid

Edvard Eriksen

1913 • Bronze

The Little Mermaid is a famous statue inspired by Hans Christian Andersen’s fairy tale. It depicts a mermaid sitting on a rock and symbolizes dreams, love, and sacrifice.
