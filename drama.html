DRAMA

Explore how playwrights and performers bring stories to life through dialogue, movement, conflict, and emotion.

THE COLLECTION

Five Playable Drama

Each dramatic work presents a unique way of exploring human experiences through performance, emotion, dialogue, and storytelling.

01 / 05

Hamlet

William Shakespeare

1601 • Tragedy

Hamlet is a tragedy about Prince Hamlet, who seeks revenge after discovering that his father was murdered by his uncle. The play explores revenge, family, betrayal, madness, and difficult decisions.

02 / 05

A Doll's House

Henrik Ibsen

1879 • Realist Drama

A Doll's House is a drama about Nora, a woman who begins to question her marriage and her role in her family. The story focuses on independence, identity, and society's expectations.

03 / 05

The Importance Of Being Earnest

Oscar Wilde

1895 • Absurdist Drama

The Importance of Being Earnest is a comedy about two men who use false identities to escape social responsibilities. It uses humor, irony, and satire to criticize social expectations and relationships.

04 / 05

Romeo and Juliet

William Shakespeare

2003 • Comedy

Romeo and Juliet is a tragic love story about two young people from rival families. Their love faces family conflict and eventually leads to a tragic ending. It explores love, conflict, sacrifice, and fate.

05 / 05

Oedipus Rex

Sophocles

429 BC • Tragedy

Oedipus Rex is an ancient Greek tragedy about King Oedipus, who tries to discover the truth about a terrible prophecy. The play focuses on fate, identity, truth, and the consequences of one's actions.
