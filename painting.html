THE COLLECTION

Six Paintings

Each painting reveals a different way artists express ideas and emotions.

01 / 06

The Starry Night

Vincent Van Gogh

1889 • Oil on canvas

An expressive night landscape filled with swirling forms and vivid colors.

02 / 05

Mona Lisa

Leonardo da Vinci

c. 1503–1506 • Oil on poplar

A Renaissance portrait known for its subtle expression and detailed composition.

03 / 06

Water lilies

Claude Monet

1890s–1920s • Oil on canvas

A series exploring reflections, water, light, and harmony through impressionist painting.

04 / 06

Les Demoiselles d'Avignon

Pablo Picasso

1907 • Oil on canvas

A groundbreaking painting that challenged traditional approaches to form and perspective.

05 / 06

The Scream

Edvard Munch

1893 • Tempera and pastel

An iconic artwork that uses color and distorted forms to convey intense emotion.

06 / 06

Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer

c. 1665 • Oil on canvas

A famous portrait study of light, color, and the subject's expression.
