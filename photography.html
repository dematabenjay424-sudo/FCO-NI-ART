THE COLLECTION

PHOTOGRAPHY

Explore five remarkable photographs and discover how artists use light, composition, perspective, and timing to capture meaningful moments and express ideas.

01 / 05

Afghan Girl

Steve McCurry

December 1984 • Nasir Bagh refugee camp, Pakistan

Afghan Girl is a famous portrait taken by Steve McCurry in December 1984 at a refugee camp near Peshawar, Pakistan. It became a powerful symbol of refugees and the human cost of war.

02 / 05

Behind the Gare Saint-Lazare

Henri Cartier-Bresson

1932 • Paris, France

A famous photograph showing a man jumping over a puddle behind a railway station in Paris. It is an important example of Henri Cartier-Bresson's idea of the decisive moment.

03 / 05

Dovima with Elephants

Richard Avedon

August 1955 • Cirque d’Hiver, Paris

A famous fashion photograph showing model Dovima wearing a Dior evening gown while posing between two elephants at the Cirque d’Hiver in Paris.

04 / 05

Lunch Atop a Skyscraper

Unknown / Uncredited

September 20, 1932 • Photograph

A famous black-and-white photograph showing construction workers sitting on a steel beam high above New York City.

05 / 05

Migrant Mother

Dorothea Lange

March 1936 • Nipomo, California

Migrant Mother was taken by Dorothea Lange in March 1936 in Nipomo, California. It portrays poverty and hardship during the Great Depression while emphasizing a mother's worry, strength, and care for her children.
